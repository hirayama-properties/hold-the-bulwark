HOW TO PLAY

How to Play

STEP 01

Tap to Summon

Tap the screen to call allies onto your defense line.

STEP 02

Drag to Merge

Drop an ally onto another of the same level to merge and level up—10 levels in all.

STEP 03

Hold the Line

Allies attack on their own. Choose where and when to place them, and meet the legion head-on.

FAQ

FAQ

Is it free to play?

Yes. The game is free to play. You can optionally watch ads for bonuses, and optional in-app purchases are available.

Can I play on iPhone?

The game is currently available on Android (Google Play). An iOS version is in preparation.

Can I play offline?

Yes, you can play offline. The global ranking, ads, and in-app purchases require an internet connection.

Do I need to pay to clear the game?

Special items drop for free when you defeat enemies. Watching ads and in-app purchases are entirely optional.

Can I transfer my save data?

Sorry, transferring save data is not currently supported.

Where do I report a bug?

Our contact details are listed on the Specified Commercial Transactions Act page linked at the bottom of this site. Including your device model, OS version, and what happened helps us investigate.
